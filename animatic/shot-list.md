## Flags for your review

**Fit (from the brief)**

1. **Target length is my assumption: 60 s.** It's an ad-style spot for the WordPress.org listing. Say so if you want a 30 s cutdown or a 90 s version.
2. **No hard fails.** The VO fits, every on-screen text passes the readability check, and the product shows early. The tightest spots:
   - S05–S07, the montage beats, run 2.5 s each. S05 has only 0.2 s of reading slack.
   - S08's bark line also has 0.2 s of slack.
   - The S16 end card has 0.2 s of slack for its copy. Most of that copy is fine print.
   - S03's VO ends 0.39 s before the cut.
3. **Sound-off viewing:** S01–S02, the hook, carry no on-screen text. A muted viewer misses "This isn't someone browsing." Option: add the line as a super.
4. **Logo timing:** the name is spoken at 0:04.5, and the wordmark shows small in the cockpit top bar from 0:04. The full logo only lands at 0:09.5. To get the brand on screen sooner, add a small logo bug from 0:04, or trim S02.

**Production risks to settle before the shoot**

5. **Readout numbers.** These captures came from the dev-tools discharge simulator, which showed 840 %/h and a postcard reading 29 → 37 %. The final needs a real 1× run on an unplugged laptop in Chrome, about 30–60 min, for believable readouts and a real postcard.
6. **Third-party video.** The bundled video page embeds someone else's YouTube video ("Me at the zoo"). Shoot S06 with our own or licensed footage, for example through a custom course page.
7. **Real-brand headlines.** The link hub's fictional headlines name real brands (Samsung, Chrome, TSMC…). Frame S08 so they can't be read, or use a custom page.
8. **Admin toolbar.** The WordPress admin toolbar shows inside the surf viewport. Hide it for the capture user ("Show Toolbar when viewing site": off).
9. **Final audio.** The temp music is a synthesized placeholder. Decide between a licensed track and an original score, and between a human voice-over and a premium TTS voice.
10. **YouTube thumbnail.** The first frame is a text-heavy article, which makes a weak thumbnail. Set a custom one, such as the logo or the cockpit.
11. **Easter egg.** S16's shark-fin wink hints at the cockpit's Konami secret, which the README keeps hidden. Keep it or drop it?
12. **Claim check.** "Most battery tests flip pages on a timer" is backed by `docs/knowledge-base/benchmark-methodologies.md`: Notebookcheck uses 30 s per page, Chromium's power_LoadTest 60 s per page, and Laptop Mag fixed dwell times. No competitor is named on screen.
13. **CTA.** The plugin isn't on WordPress.org yet (that release is postponed). The end card says "Free WordPress plugin" with no URL, since viewers will already be on the listing.

## How this animatic was made

- **Frames:** real captures from a local WordPress Playground run, put through a pencil filter. Motion-graphics shots are hand-drawn sketches.
- **VO:** macOS `say`, voice "Ava (Premium)", 175 wpm. Leading and trailing silence was trimmed, then each line was measured.
- **Music:** original synth, 120 BPM.
- **SFX:** synthesized. The finish chime reproduces `src/cockpit/FinishChime.ts` exactly.
- **Mix:** −16 LUFS, with the music ducked under the VO.
