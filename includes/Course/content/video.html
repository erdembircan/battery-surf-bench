Watch: Inside a Modern Laptop's Power Path

What actually happens inside a laptop when you open a browser tab? In this eighteen-minute documentary, we follow the electrical path from battery cell to pixel — tracing how milliwatts of stored energy are transformed, routed, and ultimately consumed by every component in the system. Along the way we speak with power-delivery engineers, a chip architect who worked on the silicon voltage regulator that governs your CPU's performance states, and a battery chemist explaining why that 100% charge reading is always a polite lie.

The film is a companion to our written series on browser energy consumption and was produced in collaboration with the Open Hardware Monitor project. All power measurements shown were captured with the same inline meter methodology described in our long-form feature. If you'd prefer the numbers in table form, see What Drains a Laptop Battery, Ranked.
